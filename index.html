Case method

1. Frame the system before acting.

2. Separate observations from explanations.

3. Choose actions for both control and information value.

4. Protect hard constraints and future option value.

5. Reassess after feedback; do not defend the first model.

6. Prefer robust policies over lucky outcomes.
